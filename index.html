PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

Apex Vision Studios ("we," "our," or "us") operates the virtual reality video game SCP:Breach (the "Game"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play SCP:Breach on Meta Horizon OS / Meta Quest devices.

By installing and playing the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a high-quality VR experience and comply with Meta’s platform policies, we limit our data collection to what is strictly necessary.

A. Information Provided by Meta (Platform Data)

When you play SCP:Breach, Meta shares certain Meta Horizon profile information with us so you can log in, track your progress, and interact with other players:

  • User IDs & Account Identifiers: Your unique Meta user ID, Meta account ID, and Oculus ID.
  • Meta Horizon Profile Data: Your username, display name, avatar, and profile picture.
  • Social Graph (Optional): A list of your followers or people you follow (only if requested by game features and permitted by you).

B. Gameplay and Performance Data

We collect anonymized technical and interaction data directly through gameplay to optimize performance:

  • Game Progress: High scores, unlocked achievements, in-game currency, and inventory.
  • Hardware and System Information: Device model (e.g., Meta Quest 3, Quest Pro), operating system version, and system language.
  • Performance Metrics: Crash logs, frame rate data, error reports, and load times.

C. Immersive and Sensor Data (VR-Specific Data)

Meta Quest devices utilize advanced hardware sensors. We explicitly handle this data according to Meta’s security standards:

  • Spatial & Guardian Data: The Game relies on Meta’s tracking systems to calculate player movement and guardian boundaries. This processing occurs entirely local to your headset; we do not collect, view, or store raw spatial, boundary, or environmental data.
  • Movement Tracking: We receive transformed numerical data (head and controller tracking coordinates) to animate your hands and body in real-time. This is not recorded or stored long-term.
  • Microphone Data: If you use the in-game voice chat features, audio data is transmitted between players in real-time. We do not record, store, or monitor your private voice data on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To Provide the Game: Authentic game mechanics, saving progress, and hosting multiplayer match sessions.
  • To Optimize Performance: Fixing bugs, preventing crashes, and improving headset battery or rendering performance.
  • To Enforce Security and Fairness: Preventing cheating, exploiting, or behavior that violates Meta’s Code of Conduct.

Data Selling Restriction: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or user data to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We only share data with third parties in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may use trusted third-party cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS, Photon Engine, Unity Gaming Services) to host multiplayer servers or store save files. These partners are legally bound to protect your data.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

We retain your game data only for as long as necessary to provide you with the game experience.

  • Your Rights: You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of any personal data we hold about you.
  • How to Request Deletion: You can request that your account data be completely removed at any time by contacting us at lukethemonkevr@gmail.com. Upon receiving a valid request, we will process your deletion within the timeframe required by Meta and regional privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).

5. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with Meta’s Developer Data Use Policy. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Apex Vision Studios ("we," "our," or "us") operates the virtual reality video game SCP:Breach (the "Game"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of players while providing a secure and immersive VR experience.

By installing and playing the Game, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a high-quality VR experience and comply with Meta’s platform policies, we limit our data collection to what is strictly necessary.

A. Information Provided by Meta (Platform Data)

When you play SCP:Breach, Meta shares certain Meta Horizon profile information with us so you can log in, track your progress, and interact with other players:

  • User IDs & Account Identifiers: Your unique Meta user ID, Meta account ID, and Oculus ID.
  • Meta Horizon Profile Data: Your username, display name, avatar, and profile picture.
  • Social Graph (Optional): A list of your followers or people you follow (only if requested by game features and permitted by you).

Additional Meta App Permissions

In some cases, the Game may request access to additional Meta Horizon permissions to enable specific features and platform functionality. These permissions are only used for the features you choose to use and are processed in accordance with Meta's platform requirements and our privacy practices.

  • User ID: Grants an app access to user ID to enable various features.
  • User profile: Grants an app access to the Horizon username and profile photo.
  • User age group: Grants an app access to user age group data from Meta. Request if your app is for mixed ages (under 13 or applicable age in user's region, and 13+).
  • In-app purchases and/or downloadable content: Grants an app access to do in-app purchases.
  • Subscriptions: Grants an app offering a subscription access to related data, including billing period start time, end time, and subscription status.
  • Avatars: Grants an app access to Meta Avatars, a persistent identity across the Meta Horizon ecosystem. With Meta Avatars, users can bring their own visual identities into your app. You must integrate the Meta Avatars SDK in order to enable this feature.
  • Deep linking: Grants an app access to view a user's desired destination within an app.
  • Friends: Grants an app access to a user's friends list. This is needed to enable certain platform features.
  • Blocked users: Grants an app access to a user's block list.
  • Invites: Grants an app the ability to facilitate multiplayer scenarios.
  • Parties: Grants an app access to Parties platform service.
  • Challenges: Grants an app access to Oculus Challenges data.
  • Device ban: Grants an app the ability to ban a device from using that app.

B. Gameplay and Performance Data

We collect anonymized technical and interaction data directly through gameplay to optimize performance:

  • Game Progress: High scores, unlocked achievements, in-game currency, and inventory.
  • Hardware and System Information: Device model (e.g., Meta Quest 3, Quest Pro), operating system version, and system language.
  • Performance Metrics: Crash logs, frame rate data, error reports, and load times.

C. Immersive and Sensor Data (VR-Specific Data)

Meta Quest devices utilize advanced hardware sensors. We explicitly handle this data according to Meta’s security standards:

  • Spatial & Guardian Data: The Game relies on Meta’s tracking systems to calculate player movement and guardian boundaries. This processing occurs in the background and is used for gameplay support and safety.
  • Movement Tracking: We receive transformed numerical data (head and controller tracking coordinates) to animate your hands and body in real-time. This data is not used to create a permanent profile of you beyond gameplay functionality.
  • Microphone Data: If you use the in-game voice chat features, audio data is transmitted between players in real-time. We do not record, store, or monitor your voice chat conversations for unrelated purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To Provide the Game: Authentic game mechanics, saving progress, and hosting multiplayer match sessions.
  • To Optimize Performance: Fixing bugs, preventing crashes, and improving headset battery or rendering performance.
  • To Enforce Security and Fairness: Preventing cheating, exploiting, or behavior that violates Meta’s Code of Conduct.

Data Selling Restriction: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or user data to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We only share data with third parties in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may use trusted third-party cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS, Photon Engine, Unity Gaming Services) to host multiplayer servers, store platform data, or provide technical support.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

We retain your game data only for as long as necessary to provide you with the game experience.

  • Your Rights: You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of any personal data we hold about you.
  • How to Request Deletion: You can request that your account data be completely removed at any time by contacting us at lukethemonkevr@gmail.com.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with Meta’s Developer Data Use Policy. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

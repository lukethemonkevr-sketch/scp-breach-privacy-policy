PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Apex Vision Studios ("we," "our," or "us") operates the virtual reality video game SCP:Breach (the "Game"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of players while delivering a secure and immersive VR experience.

By installing or using the Game, you acknowledge and agree that your information may be collected, used, and disclosed in accordance with this Privacy Policy and the applicable terms of the Meta Horizon platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information that is necessary to operate the Game, provide gameplay features, maintain platform compatibility, and comply with Meta’s developer requirements and policies.

A. Meta Platform Data

When you use SCP:Breach, Meta may share certain account and profile information with us so that we can authenticate your account, maintain gameplay progression, and support platform social features. This information may include:

  • User ID and Account Identifiers: Your unique Meta user ID, account ID, and related platform identifiers necessary for login, persistence, and player tracking.
  • Profile Data: Your Meta Horizon username, display name, avatar, and profile photo.
  • Social Graph Data: Information related to friends, followers, or social connections when required for features enabled by you and permitted by the platform.

Meta Permissions and App Review Compliance

To enable certain features within the Game, we may request access to specific Meta platform permissions. These permissions are used only to provide the functionality associated with that feature and are not used for unrelated advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes. We process this data in accordance with Meta’s platform requirements and this Privacy Policy.

  • User ID: Allows the app to access a user's unique identifier to enable account-linked features and gameplay functionality.
  • User profile: Allows the app to access a user's Horizon username and profile photo.
  • User age group: Allows the app to access a user's age group data from Meta. This may be required where the app is intended for mixed-age audiences (including users under the applicable age threshold and users aged 13+).
  • In-app purchases and downloadable content: Allows the app to process in-app purchases and related digital content transactions.
  • Subscriptions: Allows the app to access subscription-related data, including billing period start and end times and current subscription status.
  • Avatars: Allows the app to access Meta Avatars, a persistent identity feature across the Meta Horizon ecosystem. To enable this functionality, the Meta Avatars SDK must be integrated.
  • Deep linking: Allows the app to access a user's intended destination within the app and support navigation flows.
  • Friends: Allows the app to access a user's friends list when required for social or multiplayer features.
  • Blocked users: Allows the app to access a user's block list when needed for safety and moderation controls.
  • Invites: Allows the app to facilitate multiplayer invitation and party-based gameplay scenarios.
  • Parties: Allows the app to access Party platform services for real-time multiplayer coordination.
  • Challenges: Allows the app to access Oculus Challenges data for progression, event, and achievement-related features.
  • Device ban: Allows the app to block a device from accessing the app when necessary to enforce security, fairness, or platform compliance.

B. Gameplay and Performance Data

We may also collect technical and interaction data directly from the Game to improve reliability, performance, and user experience. This data may include:

  • Game Progress: Achievements, in-game statistics, progression state, scores, inventory, and saved settings.
  • Hardware and System Information: Device model, OS version, language settings, and relevant headset configuration data.
  • Performance Metrics: Crash reports, frame-rate data, error logs, latency, and load-time diagnostics.

C. Immersive and Sensor Data

Meta Quest devices may provide spatial, motion, and input data required for VR gameplay and user interaction. We handle such data in accordance with Meta’s security standards and only use it for the purpose of delivering the Game experience. This may include:

  • Spatial and Guardian Data: Tracking information used to calculate movement, room boundaries, and safe gameplay boundaries.
  • Movement Tracking: Transformed positional and movement data for head and controller tracking used to animate in-game character actions and input controls.
  • Microphone Data: If you use voice chat features, audio may be transmitted in real time between players. We do not record or store voice communications for unrelated purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following limited purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Game: Logging in, saving progress, enabling multiplayer functionality, and supporting core gameplay systems.
  • To improve performance and reliability: Diagnosing errors, optimizing device compatibility, reducing crashes, and enhancing gameplay stability.
  • To maintain safety and fairness: Detecting cheating, preventing abuse, enforcing community rules, and protecting the integrity of the player experience.
  • To support platform-required features: Enabling account linking, party systems, invites, avatar configuration, and social interactions where available.

No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or platform data for advertising, marketing, or third-party commercial purposes.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We may share information only in limited circumstances that are necessary to operate the Game or comply with legal obligations. This may include:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party infrastructure providers, analytics systems, or cloud services used to support gameplay, multiplayer hosting, storage, or technical operation.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, regulatory request, or valid governmental authority.

We do not share your personal information with unrelated third parties for marketing or data brokerage purposes.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Game, operate the platform features we support, and comply with legal or operational requirements.

  • Access and Correction: You may request access to the personal information we hold about you and ask that it be corrected if inaccurate.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your account or personal data at any time by contacting us using the information below.
  • Retention Exceptions: Some data may be retained where necessary to comply with law, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, or protect the safety of users.

5. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Game, platform requirements, or applicable law. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Game after such changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

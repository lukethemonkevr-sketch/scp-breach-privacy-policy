PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

Apex Vision Studios ("we," "our," or "us") operates the virtual reality video game SCP:Breach (the "Game"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of players and handling personal information responsibly in accordance with applicable privacy laws, platform requirements, and user expectations.

By installing or using the Game, you acknowledge and agree that your information may be collected, processed, and disclosed in accordance with this Privacy Policy and the applicable requirements of Meta Horizon and other relevant platform providers.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate the Game, provide core functionality, improve performance, maintain platform compatibility, and comply with applicable Meta developer and privacy requirements.

A. Meta Platform Data

When you use SCP:Breach, Meta may provide us with account, profile, and platform information required to authenticate your account, maintain progress, and support features available through the Meta ecosystem.

  • User ID and Account Identifiers: Your unique Meta user ID, account ID, and related identifiers required for account access and player persistence.
  • Profile Data: Your Meta Horizon username, display name, avatar, and profile image.
  • Social Connection Data: Information related to your friends, followers, or social connections where required to enable platform features and gameplay support.

Meta Permissions and App Review Compliance

To enable certain features within SCP:Breach, we may request access to specific Meta platform permissions. These permissions are used only for the functionality they are necessary to support and are handled in accordance with applicable policy requirements.

  • User ID: Allows access to a user's unique identifier for account-linked features and gameplay functionality.
  • User profile: Allows access to the Horizon username and profile photo.
  • User age group: Allows access to age group data from Meta. This may be required where the app is intended for mixed-age audiences, including users under the age of 18 where platform rules require age-based protections.
  • In-app purchases and downloadable content: Allows access to facilitate in-app purchases and related digital content transactions.
  • Subscriptions: Allows access to subscription-related data, including billing period start and end times and subscription status.
  • Avatars: Allows access to Meta Avatars, a persistent identity feature across the Meta Horizon ecosystem. To enable this functionality, the Meta Avatar system may be used to personalize the player experience.
  • Deep linking: Allows access to a user's intended destination within the app to support navigation and app flow continuity.
  • Friends: Allows access to a user's friends list where needed for social or multiplayer functionality.
  • Blocked users: Allows access to a user's block list for moderation, safety, and abuse prevention controls.
  • Invites: Allows the app to facilitate multiplayer invitation and invitation-based social scenarios.
  • Parties: Allows access to Party platform services for multiplayer coordination and session management.
  • Challenges: Allows access to Oculus Challenges data for progression and achievement-related features.
  • Device ban: Allows the app to restrict a device from accessing the Game where necessary to enforce security, fairness, or compliance requirements.

B. Gameplay and Performance Data

We may also collect technical and interaction data directly from the Game to improve reliability, optimize performance, and maintain a secure user experience. This may include:

  • Progression and Gameplay Data: High scores, achievement state, saved progress, inventory, and game state metadata.
  • Hardware and System Information: Device model, operating system version, headset configuration, language settings, and related technical data.
  • Performance Diagnostics: Crash data, frame-rate metrics, latency, error logs, and general gameplay analytics used to improve stability and responsiveness.

C. Immersive and Sensor Data

Meta Quest devices may supply spatial, motion, and input data required for VR gameplay and user interaction. We handle such data in accordance with Meta’s security standards and only use it where necessary to deliver core gameplay functionality.

  • Spatial and Guardian Data: Tracking information used to calculate movement, room boundaries, and safe gameplay boundaries.
  • Movement Tracking: Positional and movement data for head and controller tracking to animate in-game actions and input response.
  • Microphone Data: If voice chat features are used, audio may be transmitted in real time between players. We do not record or retain unrelated voice data unless specifically required and disclosed to you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following limited purposes:

  • To operate and maintain the Game: Account access, game state persistence, multiplayer functionality, and user interaction support.
  • To improve performance and reliability: Diagnosing errors, optimizing compatibility, reducing crashes, and enhancing gameplay stability.
  • To maintain safety and fairness: Preventing cheating, preventing abuse, enforcing community rules, and protecting the integrity of the player experience.
  • To support platform-required features: Enabling account linking, party systems, invites, avatar configuration, and social interactions where available.

No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information or platform data for advertising, promotional, or third-party commercial purposes.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We may share information only in limited circumstances that are necessary to operate the Game, provide platform features, or comply with legal obligations. This may include:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party infrastructure providers, cloud services, or technical vendors used to support gameplay, multiplayer hosting, analytics, and storage where necessary.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information where required by law, regulatory process, court order, or valid governmental request.

We do not share personal information with unrelated third parties for marketing, data brokerage, or advertising purposes.

4. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the Game, fulfill legitimate operational needs, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

  • Access and Correction: You may request access to the personal information we hold about you and ask that it be corrected if inaccurate.
  • Deletion: Users may request deletion of their personal data at any time. The right to deletion of user data is freely available to all users, regardless of geographic location.
  • Retention Exceptions: Certain data may be retained where necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, or protect legitimate interests.

5. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Game, platform requirements, or applicable law. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date and publish the revised policy in the Game or via the associated app channels.
